## Data Extraction Methodology

**Literature Search:**
- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Google Scholar, PsycINFO
- Search terms: "multiverse analysis" OR "specification curve" OR "vibration of effects" OR "analytical flexibility"
- Date range: January 1, 2016 - August 31, 2024
- Initial hits: 487 articles

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Published in peer-reviewed journal
2. Conducted multiverse or specification curve analysis with ≥100 paths
3. Reported sufficient data to extract/calculate fragility metrics
4. Authors provided clear conclusion about robustness/fragility
5. Not purely methodological/simulation study

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Conference papers, preprints, dissertations
2. < 100 analytical paths
3. Insufficient data reported
4. No clear robustness conclusion
5. Purely methodological papers

**Data Extraction Process:**
1. **Dual independent extraction:** Two coders (SMC, JLP) independently extracted data
2. **Metrics calculated:** IF, DF, SF, VoE computed from reported results or digitized from figures
3. **Conclusion coding:** Both coders independently classified authors' conclusions as:
   - Robust (1)
   - Moderately robust (2)
   - Fragile (3)
   - No effect (4)
4. **Consensus:** Disagreements (n=4, 12.1%) resolved through discussion
5. **Verification:** All calculations verified by third author (DRM)

**Interrater Reliability:**
- Cohen's κ for conclusion coding: 0.89 (95% CI: [0.78, 0.96])
- Intraclass correlation for IF: 0.95
- Intraclass correlation for DF: 0.92
- Intraclass correlation for SF: 0.94
- Intraclass correlation for VoE: 0.88

## Notes

1. **IF (Inferential Fragility):** Proportion of paths with p ≥ 0.05. Extracted from text or calculated from reported numbers of significant/non-significant paths.

2. **DF (Descriptive Fragility):** Coefficient of variation (SD/|Mean|) of effect estimates. Extracted from reported summary statistics or estimated from forest plots using digitization software (WebPlotDigitizer v4.5).

3. **SF (Sign Fragility):** Proportion of paths with effect direction opposite to median. Calculated from reported distributions or extracted from multiverse plots.

4. **VoE (Vibration of Effects):** Ratio of 95th to 5th percentile of effects. Extracted from quantile reports or estimated from cumulative distribution plots.

5. **Missing Data:** For 3 studies where exact percentiles were not reported, VoE estimated using range/IQR conversion: VoE ≈ (max - min) / (Q3 - Q1) × 1.35

6. **Authors' Conclusions:** Direct quotes from abstract/discussion sections. When multiple statements present, most definitive conclusion used.

7. **Agreement Coding:** "✓" indicates fragility metrics classification aligned with authors' conclusion (e.g., IF < 0.20 when authors said "robust").
